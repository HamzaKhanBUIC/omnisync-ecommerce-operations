# Skill: Problem & Pain Point Validation (`problem-validation`)

## 1. Clear Purpose & Overview
Calculates the real-world operational and financial impact of multi-channel synchronization failures.

## 2. When to Use (Trigger Criteria)
Triggered during Phase 1 by customer-analyst and product-strategist.

## 3. Required Inputs & Context
- Merchant workflow descriptions
- Oversell incident scenarios

## 4. Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Model the root causes of ghost inventory and out-of-order webhook delivery.
2. Quantify merchant labor hours lost to manual spreadsheet inventory reconciliation.
3. Map pain points to target customer personas.
4. Author research/PAIN_POINTS.md and research/CUSTOMERS.md.

## 5. Expected Outputs & Deliverables
- research/PAIN_POINTS.md
- research/CUSTOMERS.md
- product/PROBLEM.md

## 6. Quality Checks & Verification Gates
- [ ] Pain points mapped to measurable metrics (hours lost, GMV lost).
- [ ] Root cause clearly distinguished from symptoms.

## 7. Failure Handling & Bounded Retry Runbook
Cross-reference with merchant support tickets and developer forum threads to validate severity.
