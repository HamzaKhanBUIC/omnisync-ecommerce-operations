# Product Definition: Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

## 1. Primary Merchant ICP
- **Target Company Profile**: Fast-growing omnichannel D2C and hybrid D2C/B2B brands doing **$1M to $20M in annual GMV**.
- **Storefront Setup**: Active primary storefront on **Shopify** (US / Global retail) alongside 1–3 regional/localized or wholesale storefronts on **WooCommerce** (EU, UK, B2B portal).
- **Order Volume**: **1,000 to 30,000 orders/month** across all connected channels.
- **Catalog Size**: **200 to 10,000 active SKUs** (including size/color variants).
- **Buyer Persona**: Operations Director, Head of E-Commerce, or Founder/COO.
- **Key Pain Point**: Experiencing frequent oversell incidents during sales and losing hours every week in manual stock reconciliation.
- **Willingness to Pay**: **$199 to $499 / month** for guaranteed zero overselling and automated drift recovery.

---

## 2. Secondary Channel ICP (E-Commerce Agencies)
- **Target Profile**: E-commerce design and development agencies managing **5 to 30 merchant client stores**.
- **Buyer Persona**: Agency Technical Director or Lead Integrator.
- **Key Pain Point**: Clients blaming the agency when custom webhook scripts or Zapier flows fail during high-traffic client campaigns.
- **Willingness to Pay**: **$499 to $1,299 / month** for a multi-tenant agency dashboard to manage all client stores.
