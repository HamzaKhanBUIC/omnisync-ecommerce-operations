# Product Definition: Explicit Non-Goals & Scope Boundaries

To maintain uncompromising reliability and rapid execution velocity, the following features are explicitly out of scope:

1. **No Marketing or Abandoned Cart CRM**: We will not build email/SMS sequences, popups, or customer lifetime value segmentation tools (owned by Klaviyo/Omnisend).
2. **No Full Accounting / General Ledger ERP**: We will not calculate sales tax, generate financial balance sheets, or handle payroll (owned by QuickBooks/Xero/NetSuite).
3. **No Direct Physical Hardware Manufacturing**: We do not manufacture barcode scanners or physical POS hardware terminals.
4. **No Customer-Facing Storefront / Checkout Hosting**: We integrate with existing Shopify and WooCommerce checkouts; we do not host customer shopping carts.
5. **No Direct Courier Freight Label Generation**: We orchestrate inventory and route orders; we do not negotiate freight rates or replace ShipStation/EasyPost.
